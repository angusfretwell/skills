# Documents

## What it holds

PRDs, specs, RFCs, ADRs, design-review meeting notes, postmortems, runbooks, strategy pages, and personal notebooks. The why is often written here in long form before it becomes code.

## Searching

1. Search user-visible terms and the PR author's name.
2. Follow child pages and backlinks: alternatives considered, appendices, implementation notes.
3. Query meeting-note and decision databases for the same terms and dates.
4. Check the author's personal space when one exists; exploratory thinking often precedes the code.

## Good evidence

A "Problem statement" or "Motivation" section matching the target. An "Alternatives considered" or "Rejected approaches" section. A postmortem naming the target as the fix. Meeting notes recording "we decided X because Y" in the PR's date range. An ADR with real content in context, decision, and consequences.

## Pitfalls

- Specs are written before implementation and rarely updated; when the doc says X and the code does Y, record the divergence as a contradiction.
- Where several drafts exist, find the finalised or latest one and check the dates.
- The relevant doc is often unlinked; broad keyword search finds it.

## Extra fields

Per document: draft or final, the section the quote sits in, and linked pages worth citing.
